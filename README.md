While my professional background is technical, my heart has always been drawn to the creative side: I deeply enjoy art, crafting, and oil painting. This artistic sensibility, combined with my logical engineering mindset, allows me to bridge the gap between visual design and code, helping me excel at creating intuitive and well-structured interfaces.
